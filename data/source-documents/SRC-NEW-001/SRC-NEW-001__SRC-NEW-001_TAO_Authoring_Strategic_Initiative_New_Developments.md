## Planning and monitoring for the production of items

### Description

HK-dir needs a more effective way to plan and monitor the production of
assessment items throughout the content-development lifecycle.

This epic will cover the capabilities required to give relevant users a
clear overview of item production, including what content is planned,
its current production status, ownership or responsibility, relevant
deadlines, and overall progress. The aim is to make it easier to
coordinate item-development activities, identify work that is delayed or
requires attention, and understand whether planned item production is on
track.

The detailed production workflow, statuses, planning information,
monitoring views, and user interactions will be defined in the
requirements and child stories under this epic.

### Expected outcome

HK-dir can plan item-production activities and monitor progress from a
consolidated view, reducing reliance on manual tracking and improving
visibility of the item-production process.

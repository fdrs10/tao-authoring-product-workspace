Purpose

Confirm the markets that fit TAO best, define how OAT should reach and serve each market, assign ownership across the company, and sequence a single platform roadmap that protects the current OAT assessment market while creating disciplined growth options.

Executive Summary

OAT must protect its core business and expand through institutional variants that reuse the complete TAO suite. Classroom assessment as governed formative materially expands the portfolio: the buyer remains a district, state or ministry; teachers apply predefined or centrally governed assessments in the flow of instruction; and evidence returns to the institution for classroom, school and system monitoring. This is not a teacher-purchased product-led tool. It reuses TAO's strongest assets: governed item banks, public-sector relationships, standards, assessment evidence, deployment control and sovereignty.

Governed formative is the closest adjacent growth opportunity, narrowly ahead of exam-owner Certification. The recommendation is to validate both in 2027, but with different motions: governed formative through institutional design partners, official or licensed item banks and ecosystem integrations; Certification through named-account B2B sales and specialist partners. Assessment-intensive Higher Education remains a targeted option that must prove program-to-campus expansion.

Recommended Portfolio

Executive Decisions Requested

Approve the portfolio hierarchy and the principle that one TAO suite serves multiple configured workflows rather than separate products for each segment.

Approve governed formative as the first institutional adjacency and Certification for exam owners as a parallel committed motion, each with its own commercial model and 2027 evidence gate.

Confirm that the classroom user experience is part of an institution-governed offer: districts, states or ministries select content and receive evidence; teachers apply assessments quickly, are not the main economic buyer but the adoption gateway.

Approve the build, partner and retire boundaries in this paper, including partner ownership for credential issuance, curriculum content, psychometric services, e-commerce and specialist delivery services.

Create a Public Sector and Education commercial pod for Traditional and governed formative, a Certification vertical lead, shared channel and bid functions, and one portfolio council to enforce quarterly gates.

Three Year Outcome

By the end of 2029, OAT should have a more repeatable Traditional Assessment business beyond its current lighthouse concentration, a governed formative offer with reference jurisdictions and demonstrable classroom-to-system evidence, and a referenceable certification portfolio. Governed formative should either have passed the 2027 scale gate with a content and distribution ecosystem or remain a bounded partner-delivered option. Higher Education should expand only where program pilots prove institutional economics.

1 Strategic Frame and Traditional Baseline

The strategic unit is the complete TAO suite. TAO Studio and AI capabilities create, migrate and govern assessment content. TAO Portal and integration services provision people, organizations and programs. TAO Advance delivers assessments. TAO Grader supports human scoring and moderation. TAO Insights and item evidence support operational, instructional and psychometric decisions. TAO SaaS, Enterprise and Community Edition provide deployment choices and reinforce the open-standards position. Sovereignty solutions are part of the core portfolio and should be packaged as a cross-segment commercial advantage.

Traditional Assessment Baseline

Strategic Design Principles

Build one standards-based suite and configure market workflows; do not fork the product by segment.

Protect existing revenue and content migration before funding speculative adjacency features.

Prefer capabilities reused by two or more committed markets; release market-specific features behind a design partner, qualified opportunity or measurable expansion case.

Use open source as a governance, transparency and portability advantage, paired with enterprise accountability and service levels.

Own the assessment core and data model. Partner where the capability is outside OAT's durable differentiation or would create an operationally different business.

Treat content as an ecosystem with rights, provenance and quality controls rather than making OAT a broad curriculum publisher.

Interpretation

Governed formative ranks first because the economic buyer and governance model remain close to TAO's traditional public-sector base. Official or licensed item banks, centrally defined assessment windows and institution-level evidence make the classroom workflow an extension of the governed assessment system rather than a consumer teacher tool.

Certification remains a very strong adjacency because the assessment lifecycle, governance, security and psychometric needs are close to traditional high-stakes assessment. Its largest surrounding gaps can be integrated rather than built.

Higher Education wins only when OAT targets assessment-intensive programs. A broad campus quiz proposition would score lower because the LMS already provides a low-cost substitute.

The governed formative score assumes access to a trusted item bank, a production rostering path and an institutional design partner. Without those conditions, its score falls toward 3.5 and it should remain partner-delivered rather than scaled.

The teacher is the daily user and adoption driver inside governed formative, not a separate market or purchasing channel. Product-led teacher acquisition, freemium economics and a broad consumer content catalog are outside this strategy.

Full L&D authoring pulls TAO toward a mature course-production category with limited differentiation. It should not receive an independent product line in this plan.

Portfolio Capacity Guardrail

As a starting planning envelope, allocate approximately 70 percent of product and engineering capacity to the shared suite and existing business commitments, 15 percent to Certification, and 15 percent to Governed Formative. Finance and Product should replace this planning ratio with capacity and contribution-margin scenarios before annual planning.

4 Segment Strategies

Each segment uses the same suite but needs a specific customer definition, offer, route to market, partner model and gate. These playbooks are deliberately narrower than the broad market labels.

Traditional Assessment

Priority actions are to complete Studio migration evidence, codify lighthouse proof, convert New York use cases into reusable reference architecture and requirements language, maintain named-account scoring, and qualify a nonexclusive panel of content and psychometric partners. In Europe, use frameworks and local delivery partners; in the United States, use NYC DOE and NYS DOE as the replication base.

Principal risk. Revenue remains concentrated in a small number of programs while custom bid commitments fragment the roadmap. Mitigate with a bid gate, reusable solution architecture, reference rights, opportunity registration and implementation-margin review.

Governed Formative Assessment

The recommended product shape is institution-first and classroom-fast. A district, state or ministry controls entitlements, standards, approved item banks, assessment policies and evidence access. Teachers select from predefined assessments or assemble within controlled templates, launch them during instruction with minimal setup and act on immediate results. Aggregated evidence then flows to authorized district, state or ministry views. Ecosystem marketplaces support discovery, launch and provisioning, but they are not the economic buyer or the primary sales motion.

A 2027 design partnership should start with one jurisdiction, subject and grade span. It should prove the full evidence loop from official bank to teacher launch to authorized institutional insight. The gate should measure time to launch, classroom coverage, assessment frequency, evidence latency, leader usage, support cost, content sufficiency and renewal intent.

Principal risk. OAT builds classroom workflow and integrations but cannot demonstrate trusted content, teacher speed or decision value for institutional leaders. Mitigate by contracting content rights and the provisioning route before build, using paid pilots, minimizing teacher setup, applying strict data-governance rules and requiring both classroom adoption and buyer-level evidence use before scale.

Certification for Exam Owners

OAT should own the assessment core and evidence. Partners should provide credential issuance and wallets, candidate management, commerce, scheduling, specialist proctoring or test-center operations, content development and psychometric services. The first proof offer should demonstrate a real exam from content intake through badge issuance without implying that OAT owns every surrounding workflow.

Principal risk. Certification scope expands into a candidate-management, payments, test-center and credential business. Mitigate with a published capability boundary, standards-based APIs, at least two qualified providers for critical integrations and margin-tested managed-service packages.

Higher Education Assessment Intensive Programs

The entry wedge is not general classroom quizzing. OAT should select Canvas as the first complete workflow, prove LTI Advantage Deep Linking, Assignment and Grade Services and Names and Role Provisioning Services, and package HECVAT, FERPA, accessibility and security evidence. Three design partners and paid program pilots should precede broad marketplace investment.

Principal risk. TAO becomes another campus tool in a market where LMS quizzes, Inspera, ExamSoft, Respondus and Questionmark already have recognition. Mitigate by targeting programs that need more governance and evidence than the LMS provides and by using a standard eight-week paid pilot with expansion criteria.

5 Channel, Commercial and Sales Model

The segments should not share one generic funnel. Traditional Assessment and governed formative are institutional public-sector motions; Certification is a vertical B2B motion; Higher Education is a program-led institutional motion. OAT should share account intelligence, solution engineering, bid operations, partner management and Customer Success while assigning one accountable commercial owner to each market result.

Channel Architecture by Segment

Sales and Commercial Model

Recommended Sales Structure

The least-fragmented structure is a shared revenue platform with three accountable motions: Public Sector and Education, Certification, and Ecosystem Business Development. Higher Education begins as an overlay inside Public Sector and Education. Dedicated teams are added only after the scale triggers below are met.

Channel Roles and Geography

Direct sales owns customer qualification, value case, forecast, pricing and the final commercial relationship. A marketplace listing is an access and procurement accelerator, not a substitute for enterprise account ownership.

Partnerships owns partner strategy, diligence, commercial terms, joint pipeline and conflict rules. Product owns supported integration scope and lifecycle; Solutions owns one-off adapters only when the deal margin includes them.

In the United States and Canada, pair state and district account plans with content primes and Clever or ClassLink provisioning. In Europe and other ministry-led systems, combine central or framework selling with local integrators, curriculum partners and a clear sovereignty proposition.

Marketing should organize proof by buyer outcome: program confidence for Traditional, classroom-to-system evidence for governed formative, defensible credentials for Certification, and program governance for Higher Education.

Revenue Operations should define territory, lead registration, partner attribution, compensation credit and stage definitions so direct and partner teams do not compete for the same opportunity.

Named Partner Landscape

These organizations are diligence candidates, not confirmed partners. Partnerships should record competitive overlap, regional coverage, customer references, integration maturity, economics, data responsibilities and exclusivity constraints before a joint proposition is approved.

Partner Operating Rules

Use nonexclusive agreements unless a specific opportunity justifies a time-limited exception.

Register opportunities and decide prime or subcontractor roles before solution design begins.

For governed formative, keep the district, state or ministry as the contractual customer and data controller unless an approved prime-contractor model explicitly assigns those responsibilities.

Product owns a supported integration only when it has a reuse case and lifecycle funding; Solutions owns one-off delivery adapters.

The partner commercial owner is not the quality owner. Content, psychometric, privacy, security and accessibility acceptance remain with the accountable OAT function.

Price partner services transparently and measure total implementation margin, not software ARR alone.

6 Ownership and Operating Model

OAT should run one portfolio cadence with separate market playbooks. Departments remain accountable for their specialty; market owners assemble the cross-functional motion and own the result. If OAT does not have a dedicated Partnerships function, the Executive Team should assign the responsibility to a named leader in Corporate Strategy or Sales rather than distribute it informally.

Final Recommendation

OAT should not choose adjacent markets by headline TAM or assume every assessment use case is equally close to TAO. It should choose institutionally governed opportunities that reuse the standards-based assessment core and credible channels. On the corrected use case, governed formative is the closest adjacency: it extends existing public-sector relationships, official item banks and assessment evidence into the classroom while keeping the district, state or ministry as buyer and governor. Certification is the second committed adjacency because it strongly reuses the assessment lifecycle and can partner for surrounding operations. Higher Education remains a targeted option, and broad L&D authoring remains outside the direct three-year commitment. This sequence protects the traditional base, creates disciplined growth options and keeps one coherent TAO suite.

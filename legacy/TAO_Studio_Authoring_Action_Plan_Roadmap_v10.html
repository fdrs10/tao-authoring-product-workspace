Roadmap design rules

These rules govern how the roadmap is interpreted and maintained. They are intentionally separated from the executive roadmap so the primary view remains focused on categories, big rocks, timing and features.
Voice of Customer first.Each feature can record evidence from customers, prospects, RFPs, Sales and early adopters.
6 + 18 horizon.The first 6 months are high-confidence planning; the following 18 months are strategic direction. The final tail remains visible for the 3.x transition target.
Value before feature.Each item records why it matters, for whom, the problem it solves and what it unlocks.
Transition is explicit.Studio evolution, 3.x freeze, coexistence, customer migration and retirement sit on the same roadmap.
Customer transitions trace to Product.MEXCBT and other strategic programmes use shared Product capabilities wherever possible rather than becoming separate product strategies.
Commitment vs Product bet.Locked customer commitments and Product-led strategic investment are visibly distinct.
Feature level.This remains an executive roadmap. Jira owns epics, stories, engineering detail and execution breakdown.
Migration ≠ parity.Data, users, training, procedures, metadata, workflows and adoption are migration gates in addition to feature capability.
